The Green Gold Gourmet Story

Proudly Rooted in Iligan City

"Pinakurat" comes from the Visayan root word "Kurat" — meaning to shock or pleasantly surprise. What began as an artisan home recipe in Iligan City has grown into a globally celebrated Philippine food treasure.

1. Natural Coconut Tuba Sap

Harvested fresh from local coconut blossom stalks by Mindanao farmers. Naturally fermented into organic vinegar rich in natural amino acids and minerals.

2. Infused Native Spices

Steeped in small artisanal batches with hand-selected native bird's eye chilies, crushed garlic, ginger, and peppercorns for unmistakable depth.

3. Empowering Local Farmers

Green Gold Gourmet Foods Inc. directly partners with coconut farming cooperatives across Northern Mindanao, sustaining local agricultural livelihoods.
